Four steps from a new face to a full register

Register

The admin adds a person and captures 3–5 photos on the webcam.

Encode

Each photo becomes 128 numbers, stored in PostgreSQL.

Match

Live frames are compared with saved faces. Closest match under 0.6 wins.

Mark

First scan is check-in. A later scan is check-out.

Made for offices, schools and gyms

Records that filter

Search by date, person or department. Export daily, weekly or monthly reports to CSV or Excel.

One row per day

The database itself blocks duplicates, so nobody is counted twice.

Runs on your machine

Everything runs in Docker with no online face services. Face data stays with you.
