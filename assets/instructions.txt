teacher will have an attendance service which stays running so that attendance can be marked during the class. This will help prevent students from marking attendance from outside the classroom  
teachers can keep a time limit for how long the attendance is open  
for example if the class is from 9 to 10 the teacher can keep the attendance open from 9 to 9:10. Students who scan within the first 10 minutes will be marked present, while students who scan after the first 10 minutes but before attendance closes will be marked late  
after the attendance window closes, students who have not checked in will be considered absent for that class date  
the teacher can enable an optional check-out feature when they need to estimate how long a student stayed in class. Check-out will be disabled by default, so students normally only need to scan once 
the checkout is fully on teacher to enable its not default 
if check-out is enabled, the teacher can open a check-out window near the end of the class. The system will calculate the estimated time between check-in and check-out  
if a student does not check out when check-out is enabled, their attendance duration will be incomplete and will not automatically count as full credit hours  
First scan during the attendance session = check-in. If optional check-out is enabled, a later scan during the check-out window = check-out  
the screen will show "Already marked" if a student tries to mark the same attendance multiple times

tabular manner 
Situation	Expected result
Student scans within the on-time window	Present
Student scans after the on-time deadline but before closing	Late
Student never checks in by closing	Absent, calculated by the system
Student scans after attendance closes	Show Timed out
Check-out is disabled	One scan is enough
Check-out is enabled, but the student misses it	Attendance duration is incomplete
Teacher corrects a record	Save the correction and update the relevant graphs

When check-out is enabled, the system must distinguish the check-in window from the check-out window. Otherwise, it could mistake the second scan for a duplicate
